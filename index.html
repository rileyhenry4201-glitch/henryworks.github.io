Gemstone Pricing Assistant Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Gemstone Pricing Assistant ("the App") handles information when you use the application. The App is provided by Henry Global.

1. Information We Collect

Gemstone Pricing Assistant is designed primarily as a gemstone pricing, valuation, inventory, and record-keeping tool.

The App does not require users to create an account and does not intentionally collect personal information such as names, addresses, phone numbers, passwords, or financial account information.

2. Gemstone and Inventory Information

Users may enter information about gemstones and inventory items, including gemstone type, weight, price, value, quantity, notes, photographs, and other information related to their inventory.

This information is used to provide the App's inventory, pricing, valuation, and history features.

3. Photos

The App may allow users to select or add photographs of gemstones and inventory items. These photographs are used to display and identify items within the App.

Gemstone Pricing Assistant does not intentionally use these photographs for advertising, profiling, or marketing purposes.

4. Local Data Storage

Information created within the App may be stored locally on the user's device so that inventory records, gemstone information, photos, pricing information, and history can be accessed by the user.

Locally stored information may be removed if the user deletes the information from the App, clears the App's storage, or uninstalls the App, depending on the device and operating system.

5. Data Sharing

Henry Global does not sell users' personal information. Gemstone Pricing Assistant does not intentionally share users' gemstone inventory information with advertisers or data brokers.

6. Internet and Third-Party Services

Some features of the App may rely on services provided by the device operating system or other third-party services. Those services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

7. Permissions

The App may request device permissions when necessary to provide specific features, such as allowing a user to select or access a photograph for a gemstone or inventory record.

Permissions are used only for the functionality for which they are requested.

8. Data Security

Reasonable measures are taken to design and operate the App in a manner intended to protect user information. However, no electronic storage method or software system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

Information stored locally by the App generally remains on the user's device until the user deletes the applicable records, clears the App's data, or uninstalls the App.

Users can delete applicable inventory and history information through the deletion features provided within the App where available.

10. Children's Privacy

The App is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. If we learn that personal information from a child has been collected in a manner inconsistent with applicable law, appropriate steps will be taken to address the information.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time as the App changes or as legal and platform requirements change. The latest version will be posted on this page, and the "Last updated" date will be revised accordingly.
